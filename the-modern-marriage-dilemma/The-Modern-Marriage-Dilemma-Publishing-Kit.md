## 3. 500-Word Author Bio (Marketing Team Use)

Sarah Weise is a Christian author, wife, and mother whose first book was born out of real conversations — the kind that happen in women's small groups, in coffee shops, and on late-night text threads among friends who love Jesus and love their husbands but can't quite make peace with one word: *submission*.

By day, Sarah is a healthcare professional with a background in corporate marketing and operations. By calling, she is a writer who believes the seed of authorship was planted in her heart as a little girl — a second-grader already making her own "books" — and who spent years submitting that dream to God's timing. *The Modern Marriage Dilemma: and the Opportunity to See Biblical Submission in a Fresh Way* is the fruit of that patience: a full-length, deeply researched, grace-filled exploration of what the Bible actually teaches about a wife's submission, and why it is a gift rather than a cage.

What sets Sarah's voice apart is her refusal to choose between two tired extremes. She will not moralize or shame, and she will not capitulate to the culture. Instead, she meets the modern Christian woman exactly where she is — formed by messages about independence, sometimes wounded by unhealthy church teaching — and walks her, verse by verse, into the heart behind the hard passages. Drawing on her own careful study of the original languages, Sarah opens up key words most readers have never examined: *kephalē* ("head"), *ezer kenegdo* ("helper fit for him"), and *hupotassō* ("submit"). She anchors the whole conversation not in gender politics but in the submitted life of Jesus Himself — the ultimate model of strength under surrender — and in the invitation every believer receives to "choose life" (Deuteronomy 30:19).

The book is built for the way women actually grow: together. Every chapter closes with reflection prompts designed for personal journaling, women's Bible studies, small groups, premarital counseling, and marriage-enrichment settings. It is a resource a leader can hand to a newlywed, a seasoned wife, or a single woman preparing for the future, confident it will spark honest discussion instead of guilt.

Sarah brings more than a message; she brings a platform built on two decades of ministry life. Her husband, Chris, has served on staff at their church for 17 years, and Sarah has spoken from stage to audiences of more than 1,200 at national events. With a launch team already forming and deep church, family, and leadership networks across the country, she is committed to getting this message into the hands of as many women as possible — while trusting God for the growth.

Her personal mission statement captures the heart behind the book: "To live all aspects of my life with the Kingdom of God in mind in order to display the love of God to my family and the world around me."

Sarah lives in Celina, Texas, with Chris and their three sons — Jaiden, Tatum, and Cylus — still, as she says, "working all of this out in her own marriage."

*(approx. 500 words)*

## 5. 350-Word Book Summary (Amazon / IngramSpark)

**Submission. There — we said it.**

It's the "S-word" — loaded, misused, and quietly dreaded by many Christian women. In a culture that applauds independence and a church world that has too often gotten this wrong, wives are caught in a real dilemma: *Do I absorb the messages of the world? Do I accept unhealthy teaching and quietly struggle in my marriage forever? Or do I open the Word of God for myself and discover what He actually says?*

In *The Modern Marriage Dilemma*, Sarah Weise invites you to choose that third path. This isn't a defense of patriarchy, and it isn't a surrender to the culture. It's a thorough, warm, grace-filled look at God's design for marriage — written by a woman, for women, with depth instead of shame.

Across five movements — **Seeing, Studying, Learning, Embracing,** and **Living** submission — Sarah helps you first notice the "lens" you've been looking through (the world, your personal experiences, or the Word), then takes you into the original languages behind the passages that trip women up: *kephalē* ("head"), *ezer kenegdo* ("helper fit for him"), and *hupotassō* ("submit"). From there she turns to the most surprising model of all — the submitted life of Jesus — and shows how a wife's "yes" flows not from weakness but from a heart surrendered first to Christ.

Honest about her own marriage and the questions she's fielded for seventeen years in small groups, coffee shops, and text threads, Sarah never pretends submission is a cakewalk. Instead, she offers biblical word studies, personal testimony, and practical tools for the real moments where surrender is hard — so that submission becomes not a loss of self, but a doorway to blessing, protection, peace, and generational impact.

Every chapter ends with reflection prompts, making this an ideal resource for personal study, women's small groups, Bible studies, premarital counseling, and marriage-enrichment events.

If you've ever heard an uncomfortable sermon on submission and wondered whether there's more to the story — this book is for you. Come and see it in a fresh way.

*(approx. 350 words)*

## 16. AEO FAQ

**Q: What is *The Modern Marriage Dilemma* about?**
A: It's a Christian book for women by Sarah Weise that reexamines biblical submission in marriage. Rather than shaming readers or echoing the culture, it studies the original Greek and Hebrew behind key marriage passages, draws on the submitted life of Jesus, and offers honest testimony and practical tools so wives can see submission as a gift.

**Q: Is this book anti-women or about control?**
A: No. The author is explicit that it is "not a defense of patriarchy, nor a capitulation to cultural pressure." It frames submission as flowing from a heart surrendered first to Christ — a path to blessing, protection, and peace, not a loss of a woman's voice or identity.

**Q: Who should read it?**
A: Married Christian women of any age, plus engaged or single women preparing for marriage — and the small-group leaders, Bible-study facilitators, and premarital counselors who serve them.

**Q: Can it be used for a women's small group or Bible study?**
A: Yes. Every chapter ends with reflection prompts, so it works as a stand-alone study with no separate guide required.

**Q: Does it use the original biblical languages?**
A: Yes, in an accessible way. It includes word studies on *kephalē* ("head"), *ezer kenegdo* ("helper fit for him"), and *hupotassō* ("submit") to clarify what the English text can obscure.

**Q: How is it different from other books on submission?**
A: There are few recent books devoted specifically to a wife's submission, and fewer still written by a woman, for women, with warmth and original-language depth. It meets the modern woman in her real cultural tension and centers the whole conversation on the submission of Jesus.

**Q: Who is Sarah Weise?**
A: A first-time author, wife, and mother of three sons from Texas who works as a healthcare professional and has spent nearly two decades in church and women's-ministry life, including speaking to audiences of more than 1,200.
