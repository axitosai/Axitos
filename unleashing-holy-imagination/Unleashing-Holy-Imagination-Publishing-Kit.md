## 3. 500-Word Author Bio (Marketing Team Use)

Duane H. Smith has spent more than four decades walking alongside youth and young adults as they discover who they are, what they are called to, and how their lives can be lived for God's greater purposes. A youth pastor at heart and an entrepreneur by design, Duane is driven by a deep conviction that ordinary people—when rooted in love and open to God's leading—can live extraordinary, Kingdom-centered lives.

His passion is not to lecture young adults but to mentor them. Over the years he has listened to their questions, celebrated their victories, and sat with them in seasons of confusion, disappointment, and doubt. He has watched many discover a sense of purpose that changed the trajectory of their lives—and has grieved to see others drift, not for lack of ability or opportunity, but because they were chasing a vision of life far too small to satisfy them. That burden gave rise to his writing.

Throughout his ministry and professional journey, Duane has served in youth ministry, leadership development, nonprofit work, and entrepreneurial ventures, always seeking to help people align their gifts, passions, and imagination with God's redemptive work in the world. He is as comfortable talking about calling and vocation as he is about faith and formation, convinced that business, nonprofit, and creative ventures can themselves be expressions of Kingdom calling.

Duane's own understanding of God matured through his story. He grew up in a small community where the church was the center of life—an environment that gave him a strong foundation, a sense of belonging, and an early love of Scripture. When he reached college and encountered people who saw God, faith, and the world very differently, he was stretched. Rather than abandoning his faith, he studied Scripture more intentionally, asked harder questions, and allowed his understanding to be refined. That journey convinced him that curiosity is not the enemy of faith, but often the doorway to a deeper one.

His path has also included seasons of growth and fruitfulness alongside seasons of loss, failure, and rebuilding. Those experiences—rather than diminishing his faith—deepened his trust in God and shaped the message at the heart of his writing: that God is near, that He is actively at work, and that He desires to do immeasurably more through surrendered lives than we could ask or imagine.

Duane writes and mentors from that hope. In *Unleashing Holy Imagination: Becoming a Catalyst for Flourishing in God's Creative World*—part of The Unstoppable Series—he invites young adults to discover their identity in Christ, discern their calling, and learn to live as agents of God's Kingdom, deploying the resources of heaven so that lives, communities, and cultures may flourish.

Above all, Duane wants the young adults he serves to know that their lives are not accidental, that they are deeply loved by God, and that He has placed a divine calling within them.

## 5. 350-Word Book Summary (Amazon & IngramSpark)

Is there more to life than chasing achievement, security, and approval?

Most young adults don't lack intelligence, opportunity, or potential—what many lack is a compelling vision for why their life matters. *Unleashing Holy Imagination* offers exactly that: a formative journey into discovering who you are in Christ, discerning your calling, and learning to live as an agent of God's Kingdom in the world.

Drawing on more than four decades of walking with young adults, Duane H. Smith reframes imagination not as escapism or wishful thinking, but as a God-given capacity—shaped by Scripture and empowered by the Spirit—through which we see what God is already doing and join Him in it. This is not a book of formulas or quick answers. It is a mentor's invitation to faithful presence, courageous obedience, and radical trust in God's leading.

The journey unfolds in four movements:

- Identity and belonging—seeing yourself as God sees you, adopted as His son or daughter
- Rooted and resilient faith—going deeper so your identity and hope can last
- Awakened imagination—letting love lead you outward and discerning your calling
- Commissioned calling—being sent into God's redemptive work, from justice to human flourishing

Anchored in passages such as John 10:10 and Ephesians 3:20–21, the book helps young adults trust that God desires to do immeasurably more through their lives than they could ask or imagine. Each chapter includes Scripture anchors, honest personal reflection, reflection questions, a declaration of truth, and prayer—making it ideal for personal growth, mentoring relationships, campus ministries, and small groups.

The ultimate invitation is not simply to discover a calling, but to live as the Kingdom come—deploying the resources of heaven into real-world contexts so that lives, communities, and cultures may flourish under God's reign.

You do not need everything figured out to begin. You only need the courage to take the next faithful step.
