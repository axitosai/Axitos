## 2. What this file is for

Enough Is Enough is a gospel book about identity. Hall's picture for the false measures is the invisible scoreboard. The attached pages move from a middle school basketball practice, through Genesis 3, through performance as a false savior, to comparison. The contents then promise a turn toward creation, the gospel, and being sent. Those later chapters were not attached.

The book is teaching. It is not a treatment plan for anxiety, depression, cancer, or autism, and it is not a promise that a reader will feel better by trying harder. Hall says that in his own pages.
