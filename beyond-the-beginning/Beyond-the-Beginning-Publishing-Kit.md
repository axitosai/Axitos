### 2b. 350-Word Book Summary — *Amazon & IngramSpark*
Most leadership books begin by telling you what kind of leader to become. *Beyond the Beginning* begins somewhere much earlier—in a garden.

Long before org charts, performance reviews, and leadership conferences, a Creator was bringing order out of chaos, establishing purpose, delegating responsibility, and cultivating an environment where life could flourish. In *Beyond the Beginning: A Leader's Guide to Cultivating Growth, Culture, and Courage from Genesis to the Workplace*, human resources executive Nikki Leos makes a compelling case that one of the greatest leadership models has been in front of us all along.

Drawing on more than fifteen years leading people and culture across startups, nonprofits, and global financial firms, Leos connects the timeless wisdom of Genesis with the real, everyday challenges of building teams today. The result is neither a theology textbook nor another management-trend manual, but a fresh, story-driven guide that treats organizations the way they actually grow: like gardens—cultivated intentionally, nurtured over time, and designed for people to thrive.

Across fifteen chapters, Leos walks leaders through the full work of cultivation: leading with presence, exercising authority as faithful care, embracing the discipline of meaningful work, building and sustaining healthy culture, onboarding for belonging, training and developing people, protecting what you've built, stepping beyond your comfort zone, and finishing well. Each chapter weaves together Scripture, leadership research, and candid personal stories, then closes with practical "homework" that turns insight into action.

Written for executives, managers, HR professionals, entrepreneurs, pastors, and team builders, the book speaks directly to a workforce—now led by Millennials and Gen Z—that craves purpose, authenticity, and culture over hierarchy and titles. And while it is rooted in the creation story, its principles are written to transcend belief systems: any leader can apply them, even as faith-driven leaders discover a deeper way to connect their values with their work.

The invitation is simple and profound: stop chasing the newest leadership trend, and start cultivating environments where people and purpose flourish together. Sometimes the clearest path forward begins by looking beyond the beginning.

Ideal for personal growth, leadership teams, and small-group study.
