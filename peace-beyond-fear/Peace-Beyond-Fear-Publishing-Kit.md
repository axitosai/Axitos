## 2. What this file is for

Peace Beyond Fear is biblical teaching and personal testimony about fear, anxiety, depression, and other troubling emotions. Negroni's method is to ask why until a root shows, then to answer with one of eight principles. The attached pages name two of those principles: honest confession, and serving. The book is not a treatment plan, and it is not a promise that panic, depression, or high blood pressure will end.
