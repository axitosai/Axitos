William L. Martin—known to many simply as Bill—is an American missionary, pastor, and author whose life has been shaped by a lifelong pursuit of truth in both the seen and unseen worlds. The lights he had chased for years were, he realized, part of a larger story: the heavens declaring the glory of God. His conversion did not erase his questions; it gave them a framework, rooted in Scripture's portrait of an unseen realm, the reality of signs and counterfeits, and the call to discern the spirits.

Bill brings an unusually disciplined, evidence-minded perspective to matters of faith and the supernatural. That same rigor now shapes how he examines the claims and phenomena surrounding UFOs, abduction accounts, and modern "disclosure."

His ministry has been global. Bill served with Operation Mobilization, taking the gospel across borders and cultures, before answering the call to plant and pastor a church on the mission field in Mexico. Decades of pastoral work—counseling the fearful, discipling the young in faith, and confronting spiritual deception firsthand—have given him a shepherd's heart for helping ordinary believers stand firm in a confusing age.

In *Light in the Darkness: A Biblical Guide to Understanding UFOs, Aliens, and the End Times*, Bill draws together personal encounter and pastoral responsibility, chilling firsthand accounts and decades of study, to argue that the "lights in the sky" are not distant space neighbors but non-human intelligences of the unseen realm. More than an investigation, the book is a field guide for discernment—equipping readers to see past the spectacle and hold fast to the one true Light.

When he is not writing or ministering, Bill remains an adventurer at heart, still drawn to the mountains, the open sky, and the God whose glory they declare. He and his ministry are based in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico.
