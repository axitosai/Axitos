## 3. 500-Word Author Bio (Marketing Team Use)

Kiesha Walker is an ordained evangelist, speaker, and author whose life and ministry are devoted to helping women find healing and wholeness in Christ. Grounded in biblical truth and forged through personal transformation, she carries a compassionate message of hope, freedom, and love to women who have learned to survive while quietly carrying the wounds of their past.

Kiesha's passion for inner healing is born from her own story. She was born in Florence, South Carolina, into a world that did not yet know what to do with her. Her mother loved fiercely but battled struggles that pulled her away for long stretches; her father was an absence she could not name. Raised primarily by her grandmother and aunts, Kiesha received care and provision, but the tenderness a child longs for was scarce. She learned early to shrink her needs, to be strong and self-sufficient far too young, and to disappear in the very rooms where love was supposed to be loud.

Instability marked those years—moving from school to school as the perpetual new girl, and even a season in a homeless shelter, a place meant to protect that never felt like home. Like many who grow up in survival mode, Kiesha carried that unseen little girl into adulthood: over-explaining herself, staying too long in places she should have left, and believing love had to be earned rather than received. For years she mistook survival for healing.

Everything changed when she realized she could not move forward until she turned around—until she went back for the little girl within and brought her into the healing presence of God. In that return she discovered the truth of Psalm 34:18, that the Lord is close to the brokenhearted. What people had overlooked, God had seen all along. That revelation became the foundation of her healing and, ultimately, her calling.

Today Kiesha ministers to women walking the same road she once walked. Through speaking, writing, and evangelism, she helps them confront painful memories, break unhealthy generational cycles, reclaim their God-given worth, and step into the fullness of who God created them to be. She is known for a rare combination of honesty and tenderness—creating safe space for women to be seen, validated, and gently led toward wholeness.

Her book, *The Girl I Had to Go Back and Save: A Journey of Healing Childhood Wounds, Breaking Generational Cycles, and Becoming the Safe Place You Needed*, distills that journey into an invitation to heal. Through Scripture, reflection, prayer, and healing declarations, she guides readers to return to their inner child, gather the broken pieces, and rediscover their worth in Christ.

Kiesha writes and ministers not as one who has arrived, but as one who is being made whole—intentional, present, and free. Everything she is healing, she often says, is for the generations who come after her.

*"You were never the problem, your story matters, and it is never too late to become the safe place you always needed."*
